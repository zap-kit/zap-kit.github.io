Why Zap

  • Made for agents. Fewer tokens per program, errors that say how to fix them.
  • Kernel to script. One language for every layer, nothing to switch to.
  • Safe with no ceremony. Memory is checked at compile time with nothing written for it: no lifetimes, no annotations, no garbage collector.
  • Built for data. Parallel loops, vectors and tensors built in: AI, ML and data work with no NumPy or pandas.
  • CPU and GPU alike. A shader is Zap, under the same types and rules; zap-engine is built on it.
  • One tool, no setup. zap builds, runs, tests, formats and documents; the compiler writes executables by itself, with no C compiler or linker.

Numbers

What the programs do

  • nbody Simulates the planets of the outer solar system pulling on each other, 50 million small steps.
  • spectralnorm Repeats a large calculation on a table of numbers 5,500 rows tall, to find its biggest value.
  • fannkuchredux Takes every ordering of 12 cards, flips the top of the pile again and again, and counts the flips.
  • binarytrees Builds and tears down millions of small family trees, testing how fast the program handles memory.
  • fasta Makes up 25 million letters of fake DNA, drawn at random with set odds for each letter.
  • mandelbrot Draws the Mandelbrot fractal, a famous picture, on a 16,000 by 16,000 pixel image.
  • knucleotide Counts how often each short piece of DNA, from 1 to 18 letters long, appears in 25 million letters.
  • revcomp Reads DNA and writes it back reversed and with each letter swapped for its partner, 60 letters per line.
  • nsieve Finds every prime number up to 82 million by crossing out the multiples of each one.
  • matmul Multiplies two tables of 1,200 by 1,200 numbers, the way it is done by hand.
  • base64 Turns 100 million bytes into plain text that is safe to email, and turns it back.
  • wordfreq Counts how many times each of 10 million made-up words appears, and shows the ten most common.
  • jsonrecords Writes 500 thousand records as text in the JSON format, then reads the text back.
  • dijkstra Finds the shortest route between points on a 4,000 by 4,000 map, as a navigation app does.
  • raytrace Draws a 3D scene of spheres, with light and shadows, on a 1,200 by 1,200 pixel image.
  • exprtree Reads 10 thousand arithmetic sums, like 2 * (3 + 4), builds each into a tree and works out the answer.
  • strsort Puts 3 million made-up words in alphabetical order.
